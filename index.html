Privacy policy for Inocente

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Inocente is a boat reservation app made by Ramon de Klein (mail@ramondeklein.nl).

All of your data is stored on your device and in your iCloud. We do not have access to any of your data.

We do not run a server for Inocente. We do not have an account system. We do not collect analytics, and we do not sell or share your data.

What the app stores

The app stores the boat you keep in it:

  • People, including a name, an optional phone number, an optional Instagram name, and an optional photo
  • Days, seats, crew, notes, and the pick-up place and time
  • Settings, such as how many people fit on the boat and the default crew

On the iPhone and iPad this stays in the app on that device. A copy is synced through the iCloud account signed in on that device, using Apple's CloudKit. The same Apple ID can sync the boat to your other iPhones. In a browser, the data stays on that device only.

People you invite with Share boat receive the boat through Apple's iCloud sharing. They can read and change that shared boat. We do not see that share, and we are not a party to it. A phone that is already using a shared boat cannot share it again. Only the owner can.

Contacts

If you pick someone from your contacts, the app copies the name, phone number, and photo you select into the boat. That copy is stored on your device and in your iCloud, with the rest of the boat. The app does not upload your address book anywhere else.

Weather, maps, and profile photos

These lookups are optional features. The answers are not sent to us, and we do not receive them.

  • Weather is loaded from Open-Meteo for a fixed place in Ibiza. The request does not include your name, your contacts, or your trips.
  • The pick-up map loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap.
  • When you search for a pick-up place, or drop the pin, that search or map position is sent to Photon's geocoding service (photon.komoot.io) so the app can show a place name.
  • On the iPhone and iPad, if you enter an Instagram name, the app asks Instagram for that public profile photo and stores the picture with the person, on your device and in your iCloud.

Invites

A WhatsApp or Instagram invite is sent only when you tap the button. The message then opens in WhatsApp or Instagram, including the date, time, and pick-up place. We do not receive the message.

Children

Inocente is not directed at children under 13.

Removing your data

Delete a person, a day, or a photo in the app and it is removed on that device. When iCloud sync is on, that deletion is also applied to your iCloud copy. Deleting the app removes the copy stored on that device. The iCloud copy remains in your iCloud account until you delete the app's iCloud data in iOS Settings, or delete the records while the app is still installed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date will be shown at the top of this page.
